# Support Operations Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer support operations using Tableau.

The goal is to evaluate support team performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and understand customer satisfaction through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis is based on approximately 20,000 customer support tickets and agent-related data.

## Business Objectives

The project aims to answer key business questions:

- How many support tickets does the company handle?
- Are customer issues resolved within the agreed Service Level Agreement (SLA)?
- How satisfied are customers with the support they receive?
- Which support teams perform best?
- How often are customer issues resolved during the first contact?
- How frequently are tickets reopened?
- Which issue categories generate the highest ticket volume?

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following metrics:

- **Total Tickets** – total number of unique support tickets.
- **Average CSAT** – average customer satisfaction score.
- **SLA Compliance** – percentage of tickets meeting SLA requirements.
- **Reopen Rate** – percentage of tickets reopened after resolution.
- **FCR Rate** – percentage of tickets resolved during the first contact.
- **Average Resolution Time** – average time required to resolve a ticket.

## Dashboards

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level overview of customer support operations, including key performance indicators, ticket volume trends, and ticket distribution.

### 2. Team Performance
Compares support teams based on ticket volume, SLA compliance, resolution time, customer satisfaction, FCR, and reopen rates.

### 3. Ticket Analysis
Explores ticket distribution by issue type, complexity, and communication channel to identify potential operational bottlenecks.

## Tools & Technologies

- Tableau – data visualization and interactive dashboards
- CSV – source data files
- Data analysis – KPI calculations and performance comparisons

## Expected Business Value

The dashboard helps management:

- Monitor customer support performance.
- Identify teams that may need additional support or training.
- Detect issues affecting customer satisfaction.
- Understand factors contributing to longer resolution times.
- Make data-driven operational decisions.

## Project Goal

To transform raw customer support data into meaningful business insights that support better operational decision-making.
